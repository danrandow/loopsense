Move orchestration workflows into portable LoopSense core

WHY:

Dan requires other people to be able to run LoopSense in their own agent setup. Retrospective and iteration-start behavior belongs to the LoopSense product, not to OpenClaw. OpenClaw is one runtime adapter and must not become the canonical owner of LoopSense orchestration semantics.

SCOPE:

- workflows/README.md
- workflows/retro.md
- workflows/start-next-iteration.md
- agents/loopy/SKILL.md
- openclaw/agents/loopy/AGENTS.md
- openclaw/README.md

BEFORE:

The first retrospective workflow and an interrupted next-iteration draft lived under openclaw/workflows/, and the retro trigger existed only in the OpenClaw Loopy adapter.

AFTER:

Portable workflow definitions live under workflows/. The canonical Loopy skill owns the natural-language triggers. The OpenClaw adapter only maps the canonical workflow's role invocations onto configured OpenClaw agents, while another harness can provide its own equivalent execution mechanism.
