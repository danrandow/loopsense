Evidence-backing and certainty labeling standard for public material.

WHY:

Public credibility depends on making only claims we can back with evidence, and being explicit about our certainty level. This standard ensures every factual claim in public-facing files (gtm-v0.md, entity files, docs, posts) either carries a source link or is explicitly labeled with its certainty status. Prevents claims from being misread as fact when they are hypothesis or working assumption.
