## 1. Who is the customer — specifically

**Still not fully specific, and that is the sharpest open risk carried into this iteration** (my
iteration-0 Q2, restated by PM as "the single sharpest open risk"). Best available answer, from

## 2. Market positioning — one sentence

For the orchestration engineer who has shipped verifiable code agents and is now stuck extending
into knowledge work with no test to run, Loopsense is a composable loop topology that routes
real-world outcome signal back into the loop as the verification mechanism — unlike synthetic-persona
validation (loopi.tech) or rule-based checking in regulated repetitive work (Grep.ai), which do not
touch this problem.

## 3. Investment thesis — why now, why this

Why now: the verification gap is organic vocabulary in the wild this iteration (8+ independent
posters, trend #3 in a Fall 2026 report) — practitioners are naming the problem without prompting
from us, and no located competitor is answering it with real-outcome feedback for novel knowledge
work. Why this team: we have a runnable spec (Delivery's D1-D3) that nobody has actually executed
yet — the non-obvious insight is not the topology idea itself (others have adjacent framings) but
that we are willing to dogfood it publicly, on our own content loop, before selling it to anyone.
That is cheap to test and hard to fake.

## 4. Constraints — what is off the table

- No paid offer, no sales motion, no revenue target this iteration. Business model thesis
  (attention-first, open spec, hosted infrastructure later) is unchanged and untested; do not act
  as if it is validated.
- No unsourced public claims — Navier-Stokes swarm framing, the 17.2x error-amplification figure,
  "manufactured referee," or "solves" language may not appear in any post or landing-page copy until
  sourced (carried forward from PM's entity0-v1, itself carried from Delivery's handover notes).
- Do not overclaim the ten-post run as a quality signal. It is a **process** threshold (did the loop
  run, did a draft cite a ledger row) not a **quality** threshold (did engagement improve). At this
  audience size the read is directional only — say so, every time the result is reported.
- **What would revoke this certification:** any of PM's three counter-evidence conditions after the
  run — (a) the ledger can't be filled reliably, (b) no verdict-window draft cites a ledger row, or
  (c) Dan overrides quality judgment identically regardless of ledger signal. Any one of these is
  grounds to pivot the mechanism, not just iterate on it.

## 5. Success at 90 days — and how we'd check it

Not a 90-day target this iteration — the near-term, checkable milestone is the ten-post run itself:
5 baseline posts, 5 verdict-window posts, one ledger row per post, at least one verdict-window draft
with a change note citing a specific baseline ledger row. If that happens and the counter-evidence
conditions above are not triggered, the 90-day question becomes worth asking: does a larger n show
engagement improvement attributable to ledger-driven changes, and has GTM found even one ICP-matching
practitioner to talk to. Until the ten-post run exists, a 90-day target would be speculation dressed
as a plan.
