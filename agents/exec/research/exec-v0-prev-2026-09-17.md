# Exec & Business — Iteration 0

## Certification status

**Certified** — 2026-09-17. Low-medium confidence. Mandate given. Resources: Dan's time only.

## The certified bet

Problem: Agent orchestration engineers building multi-agent knowledge work systems hit a wall that
coding agents don't: there is no automatic pass/fail. Unstructured topologies compound error
(17.2× amplification — Bag of Agents 2026). Single loops stop at one polite pass. Build-critic pairs
improve quality but don't solve the structural coordination problem. The verification gap —
"you have to manufacture the referee" — is publicly named and unsolved as of Sep 2026.

Hypothesis: Defining agent coordination topology in the Randow Maps schema gives orchestration
engineers an inspectable, scenario-comparable coordination spec with a consideration mechanism
that functions as a lightweight manufactured referee.

Uniqueness: YAML-defined (not code), visual topology, semantic entity types (not just roles),
scenario-comparable runs, bypass loops as named tensions. Not LangGraph (code, not artifact).
Not CrewAI (roles, no topology). Not AutoGen (conversational, not artifact-passing).

## Downstream brief — iteration 0

### Who is the customer

**Primary**: Agent orchestration engineers who have shipped verifiable agents — pipelines
for code, devops, or other domains where pass/fail is automatic. Their customer comes back and says:
"Can you do this for product work?" Discovery. Roadmap prioritisation. Competitive analysis. User
research synthesis. OKR alignment. The engineer knows how to build the pipeline. What they can't
answer is: how do I tell if it worked?

They don't say no. They build it. Then they're stuck reading the outputs and hoping.

The tell: they've shipped agents that pass tests and are now building agents with no tests.
Their customer thinks they know what they're doing. They're figuring it out in real time.

They don't have a word for the problem yet — the "verification gap" is our framing, not theirs.
But they will recognise it immediately when they see it named.

**What the customer asks about (product work cases):**
- Discovery: "build me an agent that interviews users and synthesises themes"
- Roadmap: "which features should we prioritise next quarter — have the agent analyse this backlog"
- Competitive analysis: "monitor competitors and tell me what's changing"
- Research synthesis: "read these 40 transcripts and give me the patterns"
- Strategy: "what should we bet on — have it reason across these inputs"

In every case: no automated referee. The engineer is the referee, which doesn't scale.

**Not** (this iteration): knowledge work practitioners who haven't yet tried agents. That is a
later audience once the tool has practitioner proof.

**Not** (this iteration): enterprise buyers. We are at zero practitioner validation. No enterprise
sale until the open adoption flywheel is moving.

### Market positioning (one sentence)

For agent orchestration engineers who can't tell whether their knowledge work agent outputs are
any good, the Randow Maps topology is a YAML-defined coordination spec that gives their multi-agent
team structure, inspectability, and a lightweight referee — without writing code.

### Investment thesis — why now, why this

The verification gap was publicly named in Sep 2026 (AI Daily Brief). Practitioners are building
in the dark. The moment the problem is named, people look for solutions. We have a concrete
approach (the topology) and the product (the map) is already built. No one else is doing this
in open, inspectable YAML.

The non-obvious insight: the topology constraint is the feature, not a limitation. Practitioners
assume they want maximum flexibility (AutoGen, free mesh). What they actually need is structure
that surfaces errors. Constraint produces quality. That is the bet.

**Cynefin framing (certified positioning decision):** Existing agent harnesses are optimised for
complicated work — domains where correct outputs can be specified in advance and verified. Code is
the canonical example. Product strategy, discovery, research synthesis, and planning are *complex*
work — outputs only reveal whether they were right after the environment responds (market reacts,
users behave, strategy survives contact with reality). You cannot write the test before the loop runs.

The verification gap is not an engineering problem. It is a domain-crossing problem. The ICP is an
agent orchestration engineer who has crossed from complicated (code) to complex (product/strategy)
and lost their tests at the border.

Honest claim: we do not make complex work verifiable. We make the loop through it structured enough
that patterns become visible over iterations. Probe, sense, respond — with an inspectable harness.

### Technical positioning — composable loop topologies

**What we are, precisely:** A directed graph with opinionated defaults, fitted to a specific
subsystem — product coordination. Not a free mesh (too chaotic to inspect, error compounds).
Not a fully adaptive graph (cutting edge, unproven at scale, expensive to build and maintain).
Something in between: a composable loop topology.

**The middle path:**
- Free mesh (AutoGen, many multi-agent frameworks): agents route themselves. Maximum flexibility.
  Hard to inspect. Errors cascade without structural intervention.
- Adaptive graph (emerging, 2026 research): topology reshapes in response to task. Powerful.
  Still experimental. Requires significant investment to build and trust.
- Composable loop topology (Randow Maps): topology is defined in advance, in YAML, for a specific
  context. Directed. Inspectable. Opinionated. When the topology fits the subsystem well, you don't
  need to adapt it — you use it. When you need more, you compose: add an adjacent topology
  (a parallel loop), a super-topology (a loop that governs this loop), or a sub-topology
  (a specialist loop inside an actor node).

**The constraint is the feature:** practitioners assume maximum flexibility is the goal. What they
actually need is structure that surfaces errors. YAML constraint produces inspectability.
YAML composability means growth without increasing internal complexity.

**YAML enables composability:** forkable, versionable, composable by reference. The map is a
shareable, diffable, citable artifact — not just a runtime config.
