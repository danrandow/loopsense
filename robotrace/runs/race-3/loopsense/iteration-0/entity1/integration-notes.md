The controller parameters were chosen to balance responsiveness and stability: a moderate proportional gain (kp=10) provides strong correction, derivative gain (kd=2) damps oscillations, and a small integral gain (ki=0.5) addresses steady-state errors without causing windup. Sensor weights emphasize the center sensor most (2) for precise line tracking, with slightly lower weights (1.5) on the outer sensors to enhance curve detection while maintaining stability. The 'last_direction' line loss strategy allows the robot to continue searching in the last known direction of the line, improving recovery from line loss events. The geometry is preserved exactly as supplied to maintain the intended sensor layout and physical parameters.
