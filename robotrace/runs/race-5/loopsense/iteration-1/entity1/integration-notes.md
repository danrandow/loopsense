The controller parameters were chosen based on integration expertise: a strong proportional gain (kp=10) to respond effectively to line deviations, a modest derivative gain (kd=2) to dampen oscillations, and a small integral gain (ki=0.5) to correct steady-state errors without inducing instability. Sensor weights favor the outer sensors (1.5) to leverage their wider placement for better curve detection, with slightly lower weights for the central and side sensors to balance input. The base speed is set to a moderate 1.0 to maintain control precision. The line loss strategy is set to 'search_left' to actively recover from line loss by scanning left, consistent with common track layouts. The observation request focuses on key performance metrics relevant to the design intent, including line loss events and steering oscillation, to validate improvements in sensor coverage and control stability.
