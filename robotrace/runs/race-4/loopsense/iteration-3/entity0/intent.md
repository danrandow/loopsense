The geometry uses a symmetric sensor array with five sensors: three front-facing sensors spaced wider at the outer edges to improve curve detection and two central sensors closer to the body centerline for precise line tracking. Sensor size is moderate to balance resolution and noise robustness. The body is sized near the upper bounds for stability and space to mount sensors and wheels. The wheel track is wide to enhance steering stability and reduce oscillations. The wheel radius is mid-range for good ground contact and maneuverability. Overall, this design aims to reduce line loss events by improving sensor coverage and positioning, and to stabilize steering by a wider wheel track and balanced mass distribution.
