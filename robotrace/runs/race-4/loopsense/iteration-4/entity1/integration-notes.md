The controller parameters were chosen based on integration expertise: a strong proportional gain (kp=10) to ensure responsive correction, a modest derivative gain (kd=2) to dampen oscillations, and a small integral gain (ki=0.5) to correct steady-state errors without causing instability. The base speed is set to 1.0, balancing speed and control. Sensor weights are negative to reflect line sensor activation reducing error, with stronger weights on the central and outer sensors to leverage the symmetric sensor array's design intent for forward line detection and curve handling. The line loss strategy is set to 'search_left' to actively recover the line by turning left upon loss, consistent with typical track layouts. The observation request focuses on supported measurements relevant to line following performance and control quality, aligned with the geometry's intent to reduce line loss and steering oscillations.
