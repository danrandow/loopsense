The controller parameters were chosen based on the integration expertise: a strong proportional gain (kp=10) to ensure responsive correction, a modest derivative gain (kd=2) to dampen oscillations, and a small integral gain (ki=0.5) to correct steady-state errors without inducing instability. Sensor weights prioritize the three front sensors to maximize path detection accuracy, with lower weights on the rear sensors to assist in line loss recovery. The line loss strategy is set to 'search_left' to actively recover the line when lost, as this is a common effective approach. This setup aims to improve path tracking accuracy, reduce line loss events, and stabilize steering control as intended by the geometry design.
