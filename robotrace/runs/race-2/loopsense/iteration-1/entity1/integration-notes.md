The controller parameters were chosen based on integration expertise: a strong proportional gain (kp=10) to ensure responsive steering, a small integral gain (ki=0.5) to correct steady-state errors without causing instability, and a modest derivative gain (kd=2) to dampen oscillations. Sensor weights prioritize the three front sensors (1.5 each) for effective curve detection, with slightly lower weights (0.8) for the inner rear sensors to provide redundancy and improve line detection. The base speed is set to 1.0, balancing speed and control. The line loss strategy is set to 'search_left' to actively recover lost lines by turning left, which is a common and effective approach. All requested measurements are supported and will provide comprehensive feedback on controller performance and robustness.
