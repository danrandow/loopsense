The geometry uses a symmetric sensor array with five sensors: three front sensors spaced wider to detect curves effectively and two inner sensors slightly behind for better line detection and redundancy. Sensor size is moderate to balance sensitivity and noise robustness. The body is sized near the upper bounds for stability and space to mount sensors and wheels. Wheel track and radius are chosen to provide good maneuverability and stability. The mass is moderate to ensure good traction without excessive inertia.
