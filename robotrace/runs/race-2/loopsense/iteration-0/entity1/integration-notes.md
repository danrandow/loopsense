The controller parameters were chosen based on integration expertise: a strong proportional gain (kp=10) to respond effectively to line deviations, a modest derivative gain (kd=2) to dampen oscillations, and a small integral gain (ki=0.5) to correct steady-state errors without instability. Sensor weights prioritize the center sensor for precise line tracking and the outer sensors for curve detection, reflecting the symmetric sensor layout. The base speed is set to a moderate 1.0 to balance speed and control. The 'last_direction' line loss strategy is selected to maintain momentum and avoid stopping unnecessarily, leveraging the sensor array's symmetry and coverage.
